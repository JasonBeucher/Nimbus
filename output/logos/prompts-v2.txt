PROPOSITION 4
Use case: logo-brand. Create a new custom typographic logo for Nimbus, a movie and TV streaming library. This is a typography design task, make the lettering itself memorable. Exact text: "nimbus", entirely lowercase, spelled n-i-m-b-u-s. Draw an original wordmark from scratch: low, broad, soft geometric letterforms; the n and m have generous almost squircle counters; the b has a distinctive fluid bowl; the s has controlled diagonal terminals. Consistent solid stroke weight, exquisite optical spacing, subtle intentional connected rhythm but every letter legible, sophisticated contemporary independent design studio craft. Warm white single-color lettering on pure solid very dark navy #0b1020, absolutely flat vector-looking with sharp edges. Center one large wordmark with generous breathing room on a landscape image. At the bottom center, a very small secondary version of only the lowercase n letter inside an ice-blue rounded app-icon square to show the actual extracted lettering at icon size. Only those two elements. No separate symbol next to the main wordmark. No cloud, no play triangle, no orbit, no star, no swoosh, no ribbon, no generic default sans-serif typesetting, no gradients, no effects, no lighting, no texture, no mockup, no caption, no watermark. Seek confident simplicity and a recognizable original typographic silhouette.

PROPOSITION 5
Use case: logo-brand. Create a new custom cinematic typographic logo for Nimbus, a movie and TV streaming library. Exact text: "NIMBUS", uppercase, spelled N-I-M-B-U-S. Draw confident monumental condensed lettering like an original independent film distributor identity: very bold narrow vertical proportions, finely balanced small counters, engineered angular terminals, extremely refined tight optical spacing. Introduce a single thin horizontal navy incision through the upper third of all six letters as one continuous consistent line, suggesting a beam of projected light; keep letters clearly legible. Lettering solid ice blue #7cc4ff on pure solid nearly black navy #0b1020. Make the wordmark dominant, roughly 65% of image width, centered in a generous landscape composition. At bottom center show a much smaller N extracted from exactly this lettering on its own as an app glyph. Only those two elements. Strong flat vector-like monochrome graphic design with perfectly crisp edges. No separate illustrative logo, no cloud, no play triangle, no orbit, no sparkle, no ribbon, no italic speed lines, no generic tech logo, no gradients, no effects, no texture, no photographic mockup, no captions, no watermark. Aim for a distinctive mature cinema identity and a forceful silhouette.
